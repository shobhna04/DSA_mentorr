🧠 DSA Mentor

A gamified DSA practice companion that guides you toward solutions instead of just handing them over. Earn XP, keep your streak alive, and never forget a problem with built-in revision.

🔗 Live demo:https://dsamentorr-eqhaxx3d7vomq6jthvr5qn.streamlit.app/solve_page

💡 Why I built this

Solving DSA problems is easy to fake: you peek at the solution, feel like you understood it, and forget it a week later. DSA Mentor fixes that with three ideas:

1. Guided hints, not instant answers: you unlock help level by level.
2. Gamification: XP and streaks make daily practice a habit.
3. Spaced revision: problems you've solved come back so they actually stick.
   
✨ Features
🔐 Multi-user login: every user gets their own profile, history, and progress
🎯 Progressive problem help: difficulty tag first, then Nudge 1 → Nudge 2 → Structure → Solution, unlocked one step at a time
⚡ XP system: earn XP for solving problems, with less XP the more help you use
🔥 Streaks: track consecutive days of practice
🔁 Revision page: revisit previously solved problems so they stay fresh
📊 Dashboard: XP, streak, problems solved, and progress at a glance
🗂️ History: browse every question and response you've had

🎮 How It Works
1. Sign up / log in to get your profile.
2. Paste a DSA problem into the chat.
3. Get the difficulty tag, then unlock hints step by step.
4. Earn XP when you solve it. Fewer hints used means more XP.
5. Keep your streak by practicing daily.
6. Revisit problems on the Revision page.
7. Track progress on the Dashboard.
